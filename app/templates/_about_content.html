🔮 VibeSearch

Great vibes only... unless you're researching bad vibes, that's cool too! 🌟

VibeSearch is a custom-built curated search engine for academic articles. Our goal is to examine how a small AI model may be used to assist research according to the principles and values of education and scholarship.

This prototype was created to help the students in the Media & Information BA and New Media and Digital Culture MA programs at the University of Amsterdam. It is built around an AI vector space of 41 journals, with a total of around 50 thousand articles.

The project is now being developed in collaboration with the UvA AI Chat team, with the goal of allowing anyone across the university to curate and share lists of journals.

A project by Gabriel Pereira, Steve Jankowski and Nina Boelsums.

Search Types

🔮 Vibe Search (Semantic)

The mystic AI-powered search using ChromaDB and the Snowflake/snowflake-arctic-embed-s model. It's like having a psychic librarian who understands the ~energy~ of your search. When you ask about "digital art communities", it'll find papers about "online creative collectives" because it gets the vibe, not just the words.

📚 Traditional Search

The practical magic approach! It counts word matches but with a sprinkle of special sauce:

  • Title matches get double points (because titles are important, duh!)
  • Abstract matches get regular points
  • If all your search words appear together? Bonus point! ✨

Like a well-balanced crystal collection - not too woo-woo, not too rigid.

🔍 Keyword Search

The "just the facts" option for when you're feeling no-nonsense. No AI magic, no special scoring - just straight-up word matching. Perfect for those Mercury-in-retrograde moments when you need things to be exactly what they say they are.
